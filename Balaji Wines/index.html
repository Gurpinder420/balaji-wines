Our Story

Mumbai's Most Trusted Wine Destination

Balaji Wines is the biggest wines and spirits retailer from Malad West, Mumbai. Since 1986, we have been proudly serving the community with the widest and most curated selection of premium wines, spirits, and beers.

Our knowledgeable staff is always on hand to help you choose the perfect bottle for any occasion — whether you're celebrating a milestone, hosting a gathering, or simply treating yourself to something extraordinary.

From Japanese Whiskey to artisan Gins, from sparkling wines to exotic Sake, every bottle in our collection is chosen with passion and purpose.
